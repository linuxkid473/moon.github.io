How to play
  • ← → or A / D, or swipe sideways: switch lanes
  • ↑ / W / Space, or tap: jump over red tape and bosses
  • ↓ / S, or swipe down: slam back to the ground
  • Red orbs wrap you in tape and throw you. A red strip on the road shows where one is coming
  • Golden SURPLUS = 10 tax cuts. Scissors cut through hazards for a few seconds; a consultant takes the blame for one hit
  • Chain tax cuts for a score multiplier. P / Esc: pause. M: mute
