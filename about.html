About Moon Games

A calm, fast, ad-free home for 160+ free browser games — built for players who just want to click and play.

Moon Games is a games portal: it doesn't host most of the games itself. Instead it's a curated index that links out to games built and hosted by their original creators and community mirrors, wrapped in a fast, distraction-free interface with search, genre filters, favorites, and a fullscreen player.

Credits

Game library, embeds and hosting mirrors sourced from the open-source harshulgoon.github.io project (MIT licensed). Moon Games is an independent front-end built on top of that same game catalog.

Nothing here is affiliated with the original publishers of the games listed. If you're a rights holder with a concern, reach out via the contact page.
